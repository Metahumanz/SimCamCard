# Excluded Observation Categories

These were observed in CamCard but are deliberately excluded from the core SimCamCard specification unless the instructor explicitly changes scope:

1. Promotional Home content, product banners, user manual/news modules.
2. VIP/subscription/payment/advertising UI.
3. Digital business-card features.
4. Enterprise verification and enterprise intelligence/data enrichment.
5. Online company matching suggestions after OCR.
6. Nearby contacts / map-based social discovery.
7. Radar exchange and unrelated contact-import tools.
8. Multi-shot enhancement/tutorial beyond the required single-card workflow.
9. Bulk sorting/management features not needed for T01–T20.
10. macOS-hosted system dialog visual styling.

Reason: the assignment requires a bounded “Business Card Digitization and Contact Management” slice, not replication of the full commercial CamCard product.
